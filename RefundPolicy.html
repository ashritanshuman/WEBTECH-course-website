Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your educational investment. If a course isn't the right fit for your goals, we make our refund process straightforward and transparent.

Last Updated: January 15, 2025

01

Submit Request

Go to your student dashboard or email support@learnify.com with your order number within 30 days of purchase.

02

Automated Review

Our team verifies basic eligibility (under 30% course consumption and no issued certificate) in under 24 hours.

03

Instant Payout

Funds are refunded immediately back to your original payment method (arriving in 3-5 business days).

1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

At Learnify, our primary goal is your career advancement. We back every individual course purchase and annual bootcamp enrollment with a 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee. If you feel the syllabus or mentorship does not meet your expectations, you may request a 100% full refund within 30 calendar days from the date of purchase.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To prevent abuse and maintain the integrity of our verified certification system, refund requests must meet the following fair-use conditions:

  • Timeframe: The refund request is initiated within 30 calendar days of transaction completion.
  • Content Consumption: Less than 30% of the total course video lessons and labs have been marked as completed.
  • Certificates: A verified Certificate of Completion has not been generated or claimed for the requested course.

3. Non-Refundable Items & Exceptions

The following categories are non-refundable:

  • 1-on-1 Dedicated Mentor Hours: Private mentor consultation hours that have already taken place.
  • Enterprise Custom Licenses: Tailored corporate training cohorts governed by separate B2B master service agreements.
  • Third-Party Cloud Lab Credits: Specialized hardware compute credits (e.g., custom GPU clusters) consumed beyond the initial quota.

4. Subscription Plans & Recurring Billing

For monthly or annual Learnify Pro subscriptions:

  • Monthly Subscriptions: You can cancel recurring billing at any time from your account settings. Cancellation stops future charges while maintaining access until the end of the current billing cycle.
  • Annual Subscriptions: Annual Pro memberships can be refunded within 14 days of the initial subscription charge or renewal date.

5. How to Initiate a Refund

Requesting a refund is simple and can be done through either of these methods:

Method A: Self-Service Portal

Log in to Learnify > Account Settings > Purchase History > Click "Request Refund" next to the course order.

Method B: Email Support

Email support@learnify.com with your registered email, order invoice ID, and a brief note describing the reason for cancellation.

6. Payment Processing & Chargebacks

Once approved, refunds are credited directly back to the original funding source (credit card, debit card, PayPal, or Apple Pay). Depending on your banking institution, funds will reflect on your statement within 3 to 5 business days.

If you have questions about an unfamiliar charge, please contact our support team first before filing a bank dispute, so we can assist you with an instant resolution.
