1. Overview & Scope

Welcome to Learnify ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate online education platforms, interactive developer environments, and mentorship services. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, utilization, disclosure, and safeguarding of your personal information when you access or use our website, mobile interfaces, and learning applications.

By creating an account, enrolling in courses, or using any Learnify services, you consent to the data practices described in this document.

2. Information We Collect

We gather information directly from you when you register, communicate with mentors, or make purchases, as well as automatically through your platform activity.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Account Details: Full name, email address, password hash, profile avatar, and account preferences.
  • Billing & Payment Information: Payment card details, billing address, and transaction logs (processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant providers like Stripe).
  • Learning Submissions: Code repository links, homework assignments, quiz responses, and mentorship call chat logs.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Telemetry: IP address, browser type, operating system version, and approximate location.
  • Learning Analytics: Video watch completion rates, quiz timestamps, playground execution times, and course progression.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data strictly to deliver top-tier educational experiences, maintain system security, and continuously enhance our curriculum:

  • To provide personalized course dashboards, track module progress, and issue verified completion certificates.
  • To facilitate 1-on-1 mentor matching and live video consultations.
  • To detect, prevent, and remediate technical glitches, security vulnerabilities, or policy violations.
  • To send important administrative notifications, billing invoices, and curriculum updates (which you can opt-out of at any time).

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Learnify uses essential, functional, and performance cookies to authenticate sessions, remember your editor preferences (like dark mode and code font size), and analyze site traffic patterns. You can manage or disable non-essential cookies via your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Service Providers

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: AWS, Google Cloud Platform (for secure data hosting and video streaming).
  • Payment Gateways: Stripe, PayPal (for encrypted payment processing).
  • Communication Tools: SendGrid (for transactional emails and password resets).

6. Data Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security measures—including TLS/SSL 256-bit encryption for all transit data and AES-256 encryption at rest. While no internet transmission is 100% immune to risk, we conduct periodic penetration testing and continuous vulnerability assessments to safeguard your data.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global)

Regardless of your geographic location, Learnify affords all users comprehensive data rights:

Right of Access

Request an export of all personal data held in our systems.

Right to Rectify

Update or correct inaccurate profile and learning data.

Right to Erasure

Request permanent deletion of your account and related records.

Right to Restrict

Opt-out of telemetry tracking and analytical processing.
