FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick Answers to Common Questions

Find fast answers to our most popular questions regarding learning with Learnify.

How do Learnify courses and mentorship sessions work?

All Learnify courses combine self-paced HD video lectures, hands-on interactive coding labs, and weekly live group mentorship sessions. You can also book 1-on-1 project code reviews with senior industry mentors.

Do I receive a certificate upon course completion?

Yes! Upon successfully completing all required coursework and passing the capstone project evaluation, you receive an industry-recognized, cryptographically verified Certificate of Completion that you can share directly to LinkedIn.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

We provide a 100% risk-free 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee on all individual course purchases and annual tracks. If you are not completely satisfied, simply reach out to support for a prompt refund. Check out our Refund Policy page for full details.

Can I access courses on mobile devices and offline?

Yes, Learnify is fully responsive across all modern mobile devices and tablets. You can stream content seamlessly or download course slides and reference cheat sheets for offline review.

How can I apply to become an instructor or mentor?

We are always eager to collaborate with seasoned tech leads, designers, and educators. Fill out the contact form above with the "Become an Instructor" subject, and our academic talent team will review your portfolio.
